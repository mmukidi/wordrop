ℹ️ HOW TO PLAY & TIPS

🎓 NEW HERE?

• Open the LEVEL menu at the top-left and tap TUTORIAL — it walks you through every mechanic one step at a time, on a real board that can't run out.

🎮 SWIPE MECHANICS

• Spell Words (3+ Tiles): Drag across 3 or more letters in a straight line (horizontally or vertically) to form a word and clear them!

• Swap Tiles (2 Tiles): Drag across 2 adjacent tiles to swap their positions.

• Backtrack: Drag backward along your swipe path to undo letter selections.

⭐ SCORING & RARITIES

• Each letter has a point value in the corner (e.g. A=1, Z=9, Q=9).

• Blue (Common, 1pt): A, E, I, N, O, R, S, T, U

• Green (Uncommon, 2pt): D, G, L, M

• Purple (Scarce, 3pt): B, C, H, P

• Pink (Rare, 4pt): F, V, W, Y

• Burning Orange (Epic, 6pt): J, K

• Red (Mythic, 8pt): X

• Gold (Legendary, 9pt): Q, Z

✨ GLOW TILES

• Burning-Orange Dashed Tiles carry a ⚡ burst number badge — clear any single word worth that many points anywhere in that tile's row or column, and the whole row or column detonates for a big cumulative score bonus!

⚡ POWER-UPS

• 💡 HINT (-10 pts): Highlights a valid word on your board for 3.5s.

• 🌀 SHUFFLE: Scrambles all active tiles on the grid.

• 🔥 VORTEX (-25 pts): Vaporizes the bottom row when tiles get high.

💡 PRO TIPS

• Reaching 100 Points or 4 Words unlocks Level 2 and awards a timer reset!

• Don't let letters reach the top boundary (Row 13), or it's Game Over!

🎯 SELECT LEVEL

Higher levels rise 3s faster but award +50% more points per word!
