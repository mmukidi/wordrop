WORDROP — PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Summary: Wordrop respects your privacy. We do not sell your personal data, we do not track you across third-party websites or apps, and we store game progress locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Wordrop is designed to minimize data collection:

  • Local Game Data: High scores, selected levels, game sound preferences, and custom gamer tags are stored locally on your device using standard localStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Crash Reporting Telemetry (Sentry): If an unexpected application error occurs, anonymous technical diagnostic information (such as your device model, OS version, error stack trace, and timestamp) is logged to help improve stability and fix bugs.

2. Third-Party Services

Wordrop uses Sentry.io for automated error detection and crash reporting. Sentry processes diagnostic logs in accordance with strict privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA) and does not sell or share user data for advertising purposes.

3. Children’s Privacy

Wordrop does not collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The game contains no third-party advertisements or tracking trackers.

4. Advertising & Monetization

Wordrop contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero user tracking cookies, and zero behavioral targeted trackers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy or Wordrop, please visit our repository or open an issue at:
